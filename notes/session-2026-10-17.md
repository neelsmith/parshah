# Session summary — 2026-10-17

## Done

- Created `data/parshiyot.psv`: pipe-delimited table of the 54 weekly parshiyot.
  - Columns: `id|label|hebrew|parshah|haftarah_ashkenazi|haftarah_sephardi`
  - `id`: `p01`–`p54` in Torah order; `label`: transliterated name (Hebcal spelling);
    `hebrew`: unpointed Hebrew name; `parshah`: Torah reading range.
  - Citations use Arabic numerals for book numbers (`1 Kings`), `-` for ranges,
    `; ` to separate discontinuous segments (e.g. `Isaiah 6:1-7:6; Isaiah 9:5-9:6`);
    every segment names its book explicitly.
  - Ranges always give the chapter on both ends (`Isaiah 60:1-60:22`, not
    `Isaiah 60:1-22`); single verses stay as `chapter:verse` (`Jeremiah 3:4`).
- Cross-checked all parshah ranges and haftarot against `@hebcal/leyning`
  (`getLeyningForParsha`); all rows agree.

  compnov corpus (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neelsmith/compnov/refs/heads/main/corpus/compnov.cex`),
  e.g. `1 Kings|urn:cts:compnov:bible.1kings.masoretic:`. Rows in canonical order.
- Verified against compnov.cex: every book URN exists, and every start/end verse
  of every citation in `parshiyot.psv` (339 endpoints) resolves to an existing
  passage URN in the Masoretic version.
